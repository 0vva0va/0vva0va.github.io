# A Y2K Inspired Boat Racing Game

![Gameplay](media/boat_game/Gameplay_BoatGame.mp4)

## Overview

This is one of my first game concepts that I spend some time working on in Unity.

The basic concept is a time-trial racing game where your fasted time per level is recorded on the leaderboards. This involves passing through checkpoints over multiple laps.

Your fasted time for each level would be recorded in a leaderboard and give the game a competative aspect.

I attempted to keep the boat controls extremely precise while still physically accurate. The game is also very lightweight and should run fairly well across devices.

## Design

The levels themselves take after Venice. I liked the contrast between this very flashy and fast game with a old and worn city.

The game is lightly inspired by Y2K aesthetics, seen in the UI and general approach taken with the games graphics.

The music is one of the most important factors for this game concept and features heavily. Again, inspired by Y2K and other racing games.

## Uniqueness

Most racing games are obviously car-centric and the idea to use boats instead allows for more of an identity for the game. The controls use physically accurate boat physics that are tweaked slighly for player control.

With music being a big part of the identity, it is built into the game. The pitch of each song is tied to the players speed, as you speed up, so does the music.

There are also pickups in the form of letters. This was a nod to one of the jobs of speciliased gondeliers in old Venice who carried freight cargo down the canals. The letters themselves also made the boat speed increase, but they tend to be out of the way of checkpoints, creating a decision for the player.

Finally, there are lots of small graphical details added to increase that feeling of player speed which arent necessarily unique to this game concept.
